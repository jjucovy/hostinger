Film Essay

Essay Assignments

During the course of the year, you will be preparing a major research paper in which you explore the medium of film through a single director.  You will be assigned a film director from the list below (no substitutions or exchanges with other students without my permission). Over the course of the year, you are required to view a minimum of four films by your director.  After you have viewed each film, for which there are a series of deadlines, you will submit a two page comment on the film to Turnitin.  The comment should be an informal, though clearly presented, journal of your reaction to the film,  The final essay can focus on any aspect of the director and the film of your own choosing.  It is entirely up to you to make choices about how you wish to approach the subject.

Due Dates:

Your informal comments should be posted on Turnitin by the following deadlines

Film 1- November 7
Film 2- December 12
Film 3- January 8
Film 4- February 27

Length: Each journal is 2 pages.  The final essay is 8-10 pages..  Due Date- March 20
