World of Hitchcock Summer Assignment

The focus of the course, a one semester course, is on Hitchcock’s career in America.  But as an introduction to his films, all students must watch, prior to the start of the school year, two of his British movies, one silent and one sound.  I am attaching a list below divided into silent and sound, for you to choose from.  Some of these films are obscure and hard to find.  A few are quite well known and somewhat easier to get.  Obviously, the best and easiest way to find these films is Netflix or a similar service.  There are some internet online film sites that allow you to stream films and watch them at home.  You can always buy these films, but I do not recommend that unless you are a fanatic collector.  The best source of all things films in New York City is the Video Room.  All students who have registered for the course should be prepared to stay after school on the first day of school to watch the first movie of the actual course together.  If you cannot make arrangements to be there, with this timely notice, you should not register for the class.

Be prepared to discuss, on the first day of class, anything that you have noticed regarding themes, plot, the use of the camera and editing.  In addition, try to be attentive to the “politics” of the movie—that is, do the movies appear to have (they do) a point of view regarding class, gender, and national identity?
