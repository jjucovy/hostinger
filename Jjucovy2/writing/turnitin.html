Turnitin.com Registration

Registering for the site

  1. Go to www.turnitin.com.
  2. If you are a first-time user, continue to step 3. If you already have a user ID and password, skip to step 5.
  3. Select "Create a User Profile" in the upper-right corner of the screen.
  4. Select "Student" when asked what type of user you are.
  5. Enter your class ID and class enrollment password. Your teacher will provide the class ID and enrollment password for your section — these are specific to each class and change from year to year, so they are not published here.
  6. Enter your email address.
  7. Create a personal password for this site, to be used each time you log in.
  8. Enter your name, and your country and state of residence.
  9. Select "I Agree" to the user agreement.

You will only need to do the above on your first visit. To submit a paper (on this and future visits), follow the steps below.

Submitting papers

  1. Go to www.turnitin.com.
  2. In the upper-right corner, enter your email address and the password you created above, then click "Log In."
  3. Click on the appropriate class name.
  4. Find the assignment you would like to submit a paper for, then click the "Submit" icon to the right of that assignment.
  5. Enter a title for your submission, then select a method for uploading it — you can browse to the file on your computer, or cut and paste it into the site.
  6. Click "Submit."
  7. You will receive a digital receipt, including an ID number confirming that your paper was correctly submitted. Save this number for future reference.
