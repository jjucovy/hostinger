Thesis

Does the paper have a thesis?

Can the validity of the thesis be proven?

Is the thesis narrow enough in relation to the length of the paper?

Is the thesis original?

Support

Does the paper contain any supporting evidence?

Does the paper contain primary source evidence or is it simply quotes from historians or restatements of well-known facts?

Does the paper contain a variety of sources and not rely on too few sources?

Is the supporting information relevant to the thesis and develop/advance the argument?

Content

Does the paper have an introduction and conclusion that are reasonable in length and do not come at the expense of the body of the paper?

Does the paper stick to topic?

Do the sub-topics in the paper develop and support the thesis?

Grammar and Style

Does the paper follow the format for citations and writing (margins, font, headings) from the North Shore Hebrew Academy style sheet Citing Sources?

Is the spelling and punctuation correct?

Are words used correctly?

Is the paper well organized, both paragraph by paragraph and within each paragraph?

Do the sentences vary in length and complexity?

Does the paper avoid the overuse of the passive voice?
