Utopia Summer Assignment

Plato, The Republic

(You have the choice of reading it online, where it can be found at multiple sites, such as http://classics.mit.edu/Plato/republic.html, borrowing the book from a library, or purchasing the book.  If you purchase your own copy, it should be the Penguin Classics edition, ISBN 978-0-140-44914-3).  Do not rely on Cliff Notes or Monarch Notes or other cribbing resources.

Read the entire book.

Keep a journal, as you read, in which you jot down your reactions to the book.  These reactions can range from the simple (“boring”, “funny” “what is going here”) to the more sophisticated, such as addressing questions about the text (“why is it a dialogue?”  “what role does “Thrasymachus play in the argument being made by Plato?”).  The journal should be brought to classes.  There is a good chance that it will be handed in and graded.
