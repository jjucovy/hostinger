Assignments
When a reading assignment is due, students should try to identify answers to the following questions:  Are humans governed by passion or intellect?  Is personality a product of nature or nurture? Are humans born innocent or evil?  How do humans acquire knowledge?  What role do love and sex play in human life?  What is happiness?  Each time a reading is completed, students must write down THREE remarks (such as a question or a comment) that they would address to the presumed author of the reading.
