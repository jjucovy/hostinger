Mock Trial

The George Jacobs Mock Trial Club participates in the New York State Mock Trial Competition, organized by the New York State Bar Association.

The club prepares a team of students to argue both sides of a fictional civil or criminal case before volunteer judges and attorneys, playing the roles of attorneys and witnesses. Team membership and case materials are announced each year at the start of the competition season.

For information about joining, or about this year's team and case materials, contact Mr. Jucovy.

General information about the competition is available from the New York State Bar Association's Law, Youth and Citizenship Program.
