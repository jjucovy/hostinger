Progressive Era Goals

  • Were the Progressives a democratic reform movement?
    How was the wave of immigration a significant factor in the rise of and the reforms proposed by the Progressive Movement?
    35. Reform in the Cities and States. Divine pp. 675-679
    a. What did Progressives think was wrong with American society and what reforms did they propose?
    b. Why was progressive reform more attractive to middle class Americans than to others?
    36. The Republican Progressives. Divine pp. 679-687
    Would you label Roosevelt as radical, liberal, moderate, or conservative? Justify your choice.
    Was Roosevelt correct in accusing Taft of abandoning Progressive policies?
    37. 1912 Election and Wilson; Divine pp. 687-695
    Compare and contrast the New Nationalism and New Freedom programs
  • How did Progressive foreign policy attempt to impose American institutions, values and practices on the world? How did Progressive foreign policy contribute to greater regimentation in the United States and less freedom for the individual citizen?
    38. America the New World Power; Divine pp. 703-708
    Assess the foreign policy goals, methods and achievements of Roosevelt, Taft and Wilson up to the eve of World War I.
    39. Wilson and the Approach of War; Divine pp. 708-715
    Were the Americans as "neutral in thought as well as deed?"
    40. The War: Battles and the Domestic Mobilization; Divine pp. 715-726
    Summarize the impact of the war on the national economy, civil liberties, and public attitudes.
    41. The Treaty of Versailles Battle; Divine pp. 726-731
    Assess the significance of Wilson's moral idealism on his campaign to win approval for the Versailles Treaty.
