Antebellum America Goals

  • Did the Jackson Presidency reflect a new democratic spirit within the United States?
    19.  The Emergence of Democracy
    a.  Read The Historians Debate.
    b.  Read pages 281-288
    Why was this period called the "age of the common man?" In what ways is this image correct? How is it inaccurate?
    How did the political development of the time reflect the changing nature of American society? What did the Democratic Party stand for? Why were none of the other parties to endure?
    20.  Jacksonian Issues
    a.  Read pages 288-296
    What do Jackson positions on Indian Removal, Nullification and the Bank reveal (if anything) about his values and beliefs?
    21.  The Bank Crisis
    a.  Read pages 296-299 (not including "The Rise and Fall of Van Buren")
    Do Jackson's policies during the Bank Crisis suggest that he was fighting for the interests of "the common man"?
    22.  Whigs and Democrats
    a.  Read pages 299-305
    Did the rise of the two-party system reflect fundamental regional, class, religious or ethnic differences?  Or, was the Second Two-Party System merely a contest of personalities?
  • What factors contributed to the rise of utopian and evangelical movements in Antebellum America?
    23.  The Pursuit of Perfection
  • Was American Antebellum Expansion an extension of the "Empire of Liberty"?
    24.  Westward Expansion.
    a.  Read pages 335-343
    25.  Manifest Destiny and the Mexican War
    a.  Read pages 343-350
    How did the Mexican War affect American development? What role did Manifest Destiny play in the expansionist policy of the United States?
