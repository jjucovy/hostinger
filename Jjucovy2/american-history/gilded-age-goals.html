Gilded Age Goals

  • To be able to answer the questions:
    30. Politics and Society in the Gilded Age and the Populist Movement.
    a. Read Gilded Age Politics and the Turner excerpt
    b. Describe the differences, if any, between Democrats and Republicans
    c. Where does Turner believe American values and institutions come from? Why does Turner believe America has reached a turning point in its history? What anxieties does he have about contemporary America?
    31. 1896 and the McKinley Presidency. Read the Populist Party and Bryan Cross of Gold
    32. The New American Imperialism. Divine pp. 611-621; Mahan, America Looking Outward
    a. What were the chief arguments made by the imperialists for the acquisition of Empire?
    33. The Spanish-American War. Divine pp. 621-627
    a. Assess McKinley's handling of the crisis with Spain over Cuba.
    34. The American Empire: Divine pp. 627-635
    a. Summarize the arguments made for and against the acquisition of colonies.
    b. Was the Open Door policy a success?
