American History

A survey course in American History from the colonial period to the present.

Course description

This is a survey course in American History from the colonial period to the present. Aside from knowing the major factual details of American History, you are expected to demonstrate an ability to argue and write about important issues, themes, and debates concerning the history of the United States. See the full Course Description for required texts, expectations, and grading.
