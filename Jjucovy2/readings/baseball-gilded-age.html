Baseball in Gilded Age America

The rise of baseball to a position of prominance in American culture in the post-Civil War era illustrates the clash between the nostalgia of the Agrarian Myth and the realities of the new Industrial Age. Please read Unimagined Baseball, Elysian Fields, and Middle Class Paradise
1. Questions to consider How did the growth of industrialism make it feasible to create baseball leagues?
2. In what ways were baseball teams run like industrial enterprises?
3. What fears and anxieties about the new industrial age did baseball arouse?
4. How did baseball owners respond to these fears?
5. How does baseball reflect a nostalgia for the agrarian past?
6. What changes in American society are manifested in contemporary baseball?
