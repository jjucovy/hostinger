Honors American Summer Assignment

Source: Read the following books:

  1. Pynchon, Thomas — The Crying of Lot 49 (Harper Perennial Modern Classics, 2006)
  2. Heller, Joseph — Catch-22 (Simon & Schuster; Reprint edition, 1996)

Task: Submit, by the first day of school, to Turnitin.com — see the Turnitin registration page for your class ID and password — a 5–7 page book review essay on the two books. Be prepared on the first day of class to take a test on the content of these books.

Guidelines

  • The essay should have a thesis. Do not just describe or summarize the books; make an argument about what the books "mean" or what they "say" or what they "reveal" about the time period in which they were written and initially published and widely read.
  • The essay should have a compelling first sentence that is not a bland or general statement of something obvious. A good first sentence should be unique to the assignment, the books, and your overall point.
  • The structure is not the same as for a history research paper. There should be a thesis paragraph, a brief background (about the authors, the content, the reception given the books), the body paragraphs, and a conclusion.
  • The points you make should be supported with brief quotes from the books incorporated into your text. Lengthy quotes should be avoided. Include citations for all quotes.
  • Remember, the essay is only 5–7 pages. That means 4–5 of those pages should be the body that develops your thesis. If more than 20% of the essay is non-body (including the conclusion), the final grade will be lower.

Grading

  • Is there a thesis statement?
  • Is there an argument supporting that thesis?
  • Is there concrete evidence — specific cases or examples — used to support that argument? An essay with any two of these three elements is in the C range; all three earn a B range.
  • Is the essay original and creative? If so, the paper can earn a grade in the A range.
  • Is the paper carefully written and proofread, with clear organization and even style? The presence or absence of these qualities will raise or lower the final grade.

Bear in mind that your paper should be as original and unique as possible. While you may discuss your ideas with others, if several papers more or less make the same points with the same general thesis, this probably means the thesis is not very creative or original, and the grade will reflect that. Consult critical commentary from others, if at all, to inspire your own creativity — not to copy the ideas.

Estimated time required

You should not do any research for the essay. Guidelines: reading the two books, two weeks or less; writing the paper, 2–3 days.

Essays must be submitted to Turnitin. Registration information for the class can be found on the Turnitin page before the start of school. Late papers, even by minutes, will be heavily penalized.
